Features and Benefits

All of the features and benefits of the different GitLab offerings can be found on the feature comparison pages. Read more about Premium and Ultimate to learn whether these are the right tiers for you.

You can run basic scans for SAST, Secret Detection, and Container Scanning in all tiers, but enterprise-grade scanning and management features are only available in Ultimate.

Ultimate adds proprietary technology and more ways to customize scans to fit your organization's needs. Ultimate-tier SAST includes proprietary technology to suppress false positive results and track vulnerabilities as they move. Ultimate-tier Container Scanning uses GitLab's proprietary advisory database and offers auto-generated solutions for vulnerabilities. With Ultimate, you can also customize the rulesets used for SAST and Secret Detection, and manage CVE allowlists for Container Scanning.

Additionally, the Ultimate tier adds key usability, workflow, and governance improvements. The basic scans in Free and Premium only output results as JSON-formatted artifact files, but Ultimate shows new findings in merge requests and pipelines, and tracks vulnerabilities and dependencies over time. The Ultimate tier allows users to view the results in the GitLab UI on the vulnerability report, dependency list, and inline in their merge requests. With Ultimate, you can also require security approvals for merge requests that affect your security posture.

Storage Limits

Projects on GitLab.com Free have an initial 10 GiB limit on Git repository and LFS storage. Users can purchase additional storage to increase the set project limit. Projects on Premium and Ultimate have a fixed limit of 500GiB per project. For more information, see fixed project limit.

Purchased storage applies to Git repository size and LFS usage. It does not apply to other storage types, such as the container registry.

For Free users, 10 GiB total of combined Git repository and LFS usage per project. For Premium and Ultimate users, 500 GiB total of combined Git repository and LFS usage per project. Storage limits for Premium and Ultimate customers are fixed per project. Buying additional storage does not increase the maximum limit for a single project. It only expands your overall available storage. For example, purchasing 1 TB of storage will not cause one project to exceed its 500 GiB ceiling. For more information, see fixed project limit.

Advanced Capacity

Advanced Capacity is coming soon and is not yet available to buy. Advanced Capacity raises the GitLab.com rate limits for a top-level group on a Premium or Ultimate plan. It comes in three forms:

  • The Advanced Capacity Pack, a flat annual add-on for sustained need.
  • Overage, which covers occasional spikes for a fixed daily price.
  • The Enterprise Capacity Pack, for sustained need above the Advanced Capacity Pack. Our sales team works out its limits and price with you.

Advanced Capacity is available for GitLab.com only.

API requests, web requests, and authenticated Git over HTTPS requests all count. Each Git clone, fetch, or push counts as one request, and so does each Git LFS transfer. Advanced Capacity raises the limits for your top-level group. For the full list of what counts and what doesn't, see GitLab.com rate limits.

With overage off, requests above the limit receive an HTTP 429 Too Many Requests response. The Retry-After header tells you how long to wait before you try again. Nothing is queued, and you aren't charged.

With overage on, your group can keep going above its limit, up to the band you authorize. You pay that day's overage price.

Overage has three bands, priced at $100, $500, and $1,500 a day. You authorize one band, and your group can't go past it. New and renewing subscriptions start on the $100 band.

On each day your top-level group goes above the standard GitLab rate limit, you pay the daily price of the highest overage band it reached that day. You never pay for a band above the one you authorized, and days when your group stays within the standard GitLab rate limit it costs nothing. The most you can be billed is the daily price of your authorized band multiplied by the number of days you go over. For example, a full 31-day month on the $100 band costs at most $3,100.

The $100 band gives the same capacity as the Advanced Capacity Pack. If your group uses it on more than 120 days a year, the Pack costs less.

Overage will be on by default when you buy or renew a Premium or Ultimate subscription. An Owner of the top-level group can turn it off at any time. With overage off, your group stays at its plan limit, or at its Advanced Capacity Pack limit if you have one, and no overage is charged.

Advanced Capacity is sold through our sales team. Premium and Ultimate rate limits are planned to take effect in January 2027 and won't be enforced until Advanced Capacity is available to buy. To discuss it now, contact sales.

Usage pricing

GitLab Credits can be acquired in three ways:

Included credits: For a limited time, Premium customers receive 12 credits/user/month, and Ultimate customers receive 24 credits/user/month at no additional cost. These included promotional credits are available for a limited time after GA, and subject to change at GitLab’s discretion. See Promo Terms.

Monthly commitment pool: Purchase a shared pool of credits for your organization to draw from. The more credits you commit to, the better pricing you receive. These credits refresh monthly.

On-demand credits: If you’ve used all your included and committed credits, you can continue using GitLab Duo Agent Platform on a pay-as-you-go basis at the standard rate of $1/credit. Once you’ve enabled on-demand billing for your account, it will remain enabled for the remainder of your subscription term. You will be billed at the end of each month for any on-demand credits consumed.

For Self-Managed customers, to ensure access to the latest GitLab Duo Agent Platform capabilities, please upgrade to version 18.7. If you accept on-demand billing, you will be charged for use of GitLab Duo Agent Platform regardless of your GitLab version.

For more information, please visit our usage billing documentation.

Included credits are allocated on a per-user basis and cannot be shared between users.

Monthly commitment pool credits are shared across your entire organization. This pool creates an efficient way for teams to access GitLab Duo Agent Platform capabilities based on actual usage rather than seat assignments.

GitLab Orbit

Orbit is priced around a shared pool of queries that can be consumed across your organization, rather than individual query allowances or seats for each user. That pool is sold as query packs, and the number of paid GitLab users in your organization determines how many packs you need. That way, everyone has access to Orbit, and usage can flow naturally between occasional users, power users, and AI agents.

Queries are sold in query packs, which form one shared pool for your organization. Query packs are paid in GitLab Credits reserved for Orbit. Each subscription also includes graph capacity, the storage for your organization's Orbit graph.

People and agents draw from the same shared pool, and there is no separate agent charge.

Orbit is available to Premium and Ultimate customers through your GitLab account team, who can size the query packs for your organization.

No. Orbit query packs are paid in GitLab Credits reserved for Orbit, purchased separately, and can sit alongside your GitLab Flex agreement.

Usage above the included query allotment is metered so you can see it, but it isn't billed during your first year.
