Frequently Asked Questions
Can I use Canva for free?
Canva Free is available to anyone. If you want to unlock more premium tools, Canva Pro is perfect for solo creators who want to design like a pro, with premium templates, and AI-powered features that make it easy to create standout content.
For individuals, marketers, and growing teams ready to scale their brand, Canva Business builds on Pro with even more powerful tools - smarter ad performance insights, enhanced brand management, more AI access, and more. Everything is designed to help you stay on brand, collaborate seamlessly, and grow your business with ease.
Want to see for yourself? You can start a free trial of Canva Pro or Canva Business.
How does AI usage work across Canva plans?
Canva's AI tools work in two ways, depending on how they're included in your plan.
AI design tools: Some AI tools like Translate and Magic Write can be used abundantly, and don’t count towards your monthly AI allowance. This includes all ‘Standard AI’ tools for paid plans (excluding Pro Lite plan, where Standard AI draws from your monthly AI allowance). Fair use limits apply.
AI tools that share an AI allowance: These draw from a shared monthly allowance that varies by plan, and resets each month.
Your total number of uses depends on:
The type of AI tools you use (higher-tier tools use more of your allowance)
How complex your task is
Whether you use a mix of different AI tools
Estimated AI allowance by plan
The ranges below show what’s possible if you use one type of AI tool (either Standard, or Premium, or Ultra - not a mix). Completing more complex tasks with a single tool or using a mix of tools may reduce your total uses to the lower end of the range.
Your allowance by plan: AI usage is pooled across all AI tools listed below, and resets monthly:
Canva Free: Limited access to Standard and Premium AI tools. No access to Ultra AI tools or Affinity’s Premium AI tools.
Canva Pro: 10x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.
Canva Business: 20x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.
Canva Enterprise: 20x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.
If you want extra AI allowance, find more info under ‘How can I get more AI on my plan?’.
AI allowance during trial is the same as Canva Free. Access the full allowance (10x more) when you start your paid plan.
How can I get more AI on my plan?
There are a few ways to get more AI allowance:
Upgrade your plan for a higher monthly AI allowance.
AI Pass - a monthly add-on for Pro and Business users who subscribe online. Get Canva's largest AI allowance, refreshing every month. Learn more
.
AI top-ups - a one-time purchase for Business users who subscribe online. Get the same amount of AI as your monthly allowance, valid for 30 days. Learn more
.
Is Canva Pro available for teams?
Canva Pro is the best plan for solo creators who want to design like a pro and create visually stunning content with ease. Only one person can use a Canva Pro account.
If you need more advanced tools to grow your business, Canva Business is the right choice. It’s designed for both individuals and small teams, offering smarter ad performance insights, enhanced brand management, more powerful AI access, and streamlined team workflows - all in one single plan.
What is the difference between Canva Business and Canva Pro?
Canva Pro is the perfect plan for solo creators who want premium editing tools and templates to create faster and design like a pro.
Canva Business is built for individuals, and growing teams who need more AI access, enhanced brand management, smarter marketing with ad analytics, and a shared workspace for seamless collaboration to scale and grow their business. Discover how Canva Business
can support your needs.
Can I invite other team members during my trial?
If you're using Canva Business, you can invite other team members to join your team. You won't be billed at all during your trial period.
What is the difference between Canva Business and Canva Enterprise?
Canva Business is designed for individuals, marketers, and small to medium-sized businesses who want to grow their business. It includes all the premium content and features in Pro, plus advanced tools like ad performance insights, higher AI access, up to stronger brand control, collaboration features, and more. Discover how Canva Business
can power your business growth.
Canva Enterprise is built for large or multi-team organizations that need more control at scale. It includes everything in Business, plus enterprise-level features like advanced brand governance, security with SSO and SCIM, custom apps and integrations, and admin controls.
Contact our sales team
to learn how Canva Enterprise can support your business needs.
How can I request a trial of Canva Enterprise?
While we don’t offer a free trial for Canva Enterprise, you can get in touch
with our sales team to see a demo of Canva Enterprise and discuss your specific needs.
What if I work for a nonprofit?
Nonprofits can get a free subscription with all the premium features of Canva Pro, plus team and collaboration tools, for one team of up to 50 users. Additional seats are 50% off on Canva Enterprise. Apply today.
What if I am a student or teacher?
Canva Education is available for K-12 (primary and secondary) educators, their students, and qualified school districts and institutions. Check out our full eligibility requirements.
Canva Campus
empowers higher education students, faculty staff, plus campus administrators and marketers to communicate visually and collaborate seamlessly with Canva’s suite of tools.
What payment methods can I use?
We accept Paypal and credit cards by major providers such as Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. We also support other online payment systems in some countries. Take a look at this help article to learn more about payment options.
Will I be charged tax with my subscription?
The prices on this page don’t include tax. If tax applies in your region, we’ll calculate it based on your billing details and the applicable rate at the time of your subscription charge.
You’ll see the total amount, including the current tax amount, before you confirm your purchase.
Here's how it works if you're in the US or Canada:
• In the US: We apply sales tax where required, based on your billing address.
• In Canada: GST and any applicable provincial taxes are added based on your postal code.
If you are tax-exempt, learn how to submit a valid exemption certificate via the Help Center
.
How does billing work when I add new members to my team?
With Canva Business, every team member needs a paid seat. If you have an available seat, you can invite someone to use it. If you don’t, you can purchase a new seat when you send the invite. You’ll be charged straight away, even if the person joins later, but you’ll only pay for the rest of your current billing period.
If you remove someone from your team, their seat stays on your plan and can be used by someone else. To lower your bill, you’ll need to reduce the number of seats on your plan. The change will apply from your next bill.
Canva Enterprise offers great flexibility for your billing needs, including the option to set up regular invoicing. To learn more about your billing options with Canva Enterprise, reach out
to our sales team.
If I add a team member, will they be able to see all my designs?
Your designs are always private, unless you explicitly choose to share them or save them as templates for your team.
How can I contact the sales team?
For inquiries about choosing the right plan or creating a personalized package for your organization, please reach out to our Sales team via the Contact Sales form.
Is Canva AI available in my country?
Canva AI currently supports these languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, and Hindi.
What happened to Canva Teams?
Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades. If you're already on Canva Teams, you'll remain on your current plan unless you switch or cancel.
Any new paid features exclusive to Canva Business won’t be included in Canva Teams. If you’re looking for more ways to grow, Canva Business offers more powerful tools like smarter ad performance insights, higher AI access, interactive data visualization, and more.
How do I get access to Flourish that is included in Canva Business?
To access the Flourish Presenter plan, make sure you sign up for Flourish
using your Canva Business account.
If you’re part of a team and experience any access issues, please reach out to your Canva account admin or owner. Access to Flourish may have been restricted for your organization.
If you’re not currently on Canva Business, you’ll need to upgrade to a Business plan to unlock the Flourish Presenter plan. Learn more about Canva Business.
Where can I see the price of my subscription?
You can view your current plan and pricing anytime in your Billing settings. From the Canva homepage, open your account menu, go to Settings, then select Billing.
