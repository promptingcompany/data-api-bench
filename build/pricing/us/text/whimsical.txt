Frequently asked questions
How much does Whimsical cost?
The Free plan is $0. Pro is $10 per editor per month with annual billing, or $12 per editor per month with monthly billing. Business is $20 per editor per month with annual billing, or $24 per editor per month with monthly billing. Viewers are always free.
Can I use Whimsical
for free?
Yes! With our Free plan, you can have unlimited boards, unlimited workspace members, unlimited guests, and more. You can also connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or another AI agent to your workspace for free.
What types of payment do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards. For Business plan customers with more than 10 editor seats, we also offer invoicing, including wire and ACH transfers. Contact sales
to learn more.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits?
Yes, we gladly offer a 20% discount to non-profit organizations. Contact us
to learn more.
How many free workspaces can I create?
You can create 2 free workspaces and switch between them seamlessly.
Do you offer discounts for students and teachers?
Yes, students and teachers can use Whimsical
free of charge. Sign up and fill out this form
to apply.
Can I collaborate with people outside my organization?
Yes, you can share a file with anyone using a share link, or invite people into your workspace as guests. Guests are free and unlimited on every plan, including Free.
